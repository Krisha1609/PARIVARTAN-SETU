Four ideas behind the platform

Collectors already do the hard work of gathering e-waste. Parivartan-Setu makes the formal route the easier and better-paying one.


⚖️

Fair

Transparent price ranges based on material, weight and local market.

🤝

Connected

Collectors meet authorized recyclers nearby and compare offers.

🧾

Traceable

Every lot moves from lot to handover to payment with a record.

🌱

Sustainable

Safer handling and formal recycling bring resources back into use.

A collector's journey takes five steps

Each action stays within one or two taps, with large icons and short words.

📷PhotographTake a photo of the material
⚖️Add weightEnter the approximate weight
💰See the priceA price range from the backend
📍Choose recyclerCompare and pick, no forced choice
🤲Hand overRecycler confirms receipt

The material is identified from the photo, and the collector can always correct it.

Follow every lot from collection to payment

Each lot gets a unique ID. Its record holds the photo, weight, GPS, timestamp, recycler, quoted price, final price and payment status.

Made for people who work with their hands

Many collectors have limited literacy and entry-level phones. The app is designed around that.

हिंदीमराठीEnglish
  • Large text and touch targets
  • Icons and words together
  • Speaker buttons for audio guidance
  • Clear online and offline status
📴

Works without internet

Add e-waste, save photos, enter weight and create a lot offline. Data is stored on the phone, and syncs when the connection returns.

Three roles, one connected system


🧑‍🔧

Collector

Adds lots, checks prices, finds recyclers, tracks earnings, reads safety guidance and keeps records.

🏭

Recycler

Shows authorization and accepted materials, reviews requests, accepts or rejects, confirms received material and final price.

🛡️

Admin

Verifies recyclers, manages users, materials and price data, and reviews records flagged as a potential anomaly.
